## How to Redeem Codes

### In-Game (Android)
1. Tap your **Avatar / Profile icon** in the top-left corner of the main screen.
2. Select the **Settings** (gear icon) at the bottom.
3. Tap **Gift Code** from the menu options.
4. Enter the code exactly as shown (codes are case-sensitive) and tap **Redeem**.
5. Check your in-game mailbox to collect the items.

### Web Redemption Portal (iOS & Android)
1. Open [Kingshot](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kingshot/id6739554056) and tap your profile avatar in the upper-left corner.
2. Note your numeric **Player ID** listed under your Governor name.
3. Visit the official Century Games Gift Code Center in your mobile or desktop browser.
4. Enter your Player ID and paste the code into the text field.
5. Tap **Confirm**; items will be delivered immediately to your in-game mailbox.

## Official Channels to Monitor for New Codes

* **Official Discord Server:** Direct code drops during developer Q&As, maintenance windows, and server updates.
* **Official Facebook Page:** Milestone codes (e.g. follower thresholds, holiday giveaways).
* **Official Instagram Account:** Visual puzzles and weekly flash codes.
* **In-Game Mail & Announcements:** Major server-wide holiday events and compensation codes.
